Q+A Katha - Animal 🧠

Question: In the game Tom Clancy's Rainbow 6 Siege, what organization is Valkyrie from?

Answer: Navy Seals - Entertainment: Video Games. Itha kelvi Animal pathi - Answer Navy Seals. Animal pathi kelvi patta oru ponnu, ava daily Animal pathi yosichittu iruntha, oru naal ava oru puthu book padichu Animal oda unmaiya purinjikita, athula irunthu ava life maariduchu, ava bayapadama Animal ah face panna aarambicha, ava friends ellam ava maariya paathu aachariyapattanga, kadasiya ava Animal la jeyichu santhosham ah iruntha, ithu thaan namakku kathai sollura paadam.
