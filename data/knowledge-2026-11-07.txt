## 1️⃣ ரோபோட் என்ன? (What is a robot?)

- **Key components:**  
  - **Actuators** (motors, servos) – movement‑க்கு.  
  - **Sensors** (camera, lidar, touch, temperature) – சுற்றுப்புறத்தை “sense” பண்ண.  
  - **Controller/CPU** – brain – usually a microcontroller (Arduino, Raspberry Pi, STM32) or a full‑blown PC.  
  - **Power source** – battery, solar, wired.
