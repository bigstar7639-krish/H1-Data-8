Q+A Katha - Marriage 🧠

Question: Who is the developer of the game "Rocket League"?

Answer: Psyonix - Entertainment: Video Games. Itha kelvi Marriage pathi - Answer Psyonix. Oru naal oru chinna oor la oru paiyan irunthan, avan manasula Marriage pathi neraya yosichittu irunthan, yaar kitta sollurathu nu theriyama thaniya irunthan, appo avanukku oru nalla friend kedaichan, antha friend avan kooda pesi avan manasa purinjikittan, kadasiya avan Marriage la irunthu nalla purithaloda sirichuttu veetuku ponan, ithu thaan Marriage oda azhagu, kashtam vanthalum nambikkaiya iruntha ellam sari aayidum nu avan purinjikittan.
Q+A Katha - Friendship 🧠

Question: What is the Linnean name of the domestic apple tree?

Answer: Malus pumila - Science & Nature. Itha kelvi Friendship pathi - Answer Malus pumila. Oru kaalathula oru oor la Friendship nu oru periya vishayam irunthuchu, yaarum atha purinjikama irunthanga, oru chinna paiyan thaan Friendship oda arumaiya purinjikittan, avan Friendship pathi ellaar kitta yum pesi puriya vachan, mella mella oor makkal ellam Friendship ah purinjikittanga, antha oor ye Friendship nala azhaga maariduchu, ithula irunthu namakku theriyura vishayam enna na, Friendship ah purinjikita life ye azhagahum.
