Q+A Katha - Marriage 🧠

Question: Who is the developer of the game "Rocket League"?

Answer: Psyonix - Entertainment: Video Games. Itha kelvi Marriage pathi - Answer Psyonix. Oru naal oru chinna oor la oru paiyan irunthan, avan manasula Marriage pathi neraya yosichittu irunthan, yaar kitta sollurathu nu theriyama thaniya irunthan, appo avanukku oru nalla friend kedaichan, antha friend avan kooda pesi avan manasa purinjikittan, kadasiya avan Marriage la irunthu nalla purithaloda sirichuttu veetuku ponan, ithu thaan Marriage oda azhagu, kashtam vanthalum nambikkaiya iruntha ellam sari aayidum nu avan purinjikittan.
